# DJI Drone Mod for Minecraft

Explore Minecraft from a new perspective with detailed DJI-inspired drones, live onboard camera views, and interactive flight controls.

## About the Project

This mod brings drone flying to Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1 on Fabric. It features the Avata 360, Mini 4 Pro, and Matrice 400, combining detailed aircraft models and animations with flight systems you can interact with inside your world.

Pair a drone with an in-game RC 2 controller, enter its live camera view, and take off to explore landscapes, inspect builds, or enjoy flying above your creations. Each aircraft offers a different look and flight experience, supported by features such as battery management, obstacle sensing, and automatic return-to-home.

## Purpose

The project aims to make drone flight an engaging part of Minecraft exploration and creative play. It combines recognizable aircraft designs with practical flight controls, giving players a hands-on way to experience remote piloting within a familiar sandbox world.

Whether you enjoy aviation, building, or discovering new views of your surroundings, the mod adds another way to interact with Minecraft's environments.

## Key Features

- **Multiple aircraft:** Fly the Avata 360, Mini 4 Pro, and Matrice 400, with detailed models and animated components.
- **Live onboard camera view:** Pilot through an interactive remote controller interface and see the world from your drone's perspective.
- **Flexible controls:** Use keyboard controls, on-screen virtual sticks, or a physical DJI RC-N1 controller connected through USB with the required DJI drivers.
- **Selectable flight modes:** Switch between Cine, Normal, and Sport modes to suit relaxed exploration or faster flying.
- **Assisted flight:** Use automatic takeoff, landing, and return-to-home, with obstacle sensing and braking available according to the aircraft and flight mode.
- **Battery management:** Manage aircraft batteries as part of preparing and operating your drones.
- **4G infrastructure:** Build directional base stations to extend control coverage beyond the direct radio link.
- **Signal-loss behavior:** Automatic return-to-home helps bring the aircraft back when the control connection is lost.

## What Makes It Appealing

The experience extends beyond simply adding a flying vehicle. You prepare an aircraft, connect a controller, monitor its camera feed, and manage its flight. Detailed models and moving parts bring the drones to life, while optional physical controller support makes piloting more tactile.

Minecraft provides the landscape: mountains to fly past, cities to survey, and player-built structures to inspect from above. The combination of remote exploration, interactive aircraft, and buildable communications infrastructure creates opportunities for both casual flying and dedicated drone bases.

## Requirements and Current Scope

- Minecraft Java Edition **1.20.1**
- **Fabric Loader**, **Fabric API**, and **GeckoLib 4.4.9 for Fabric 1.20.1**
- For physical RC-N1 control: a compatible USB connection and the **DJI Assistant 2 consumer drone drivers**

The project focuses on flight, live camera viewing, and aircraft interaction. Photo capture, video recording, and playback are not currently implemented; related interface elements are status displays. It is a Minecraft gameplay experience rather than a certified flight-training simulator.
